Multi-Storey Window Cleaning Done Safely

Height changes everything about window cleaning. Glass on upper levels collects the same dirt as ground-floor windows, but cleaning it requires proper planning, equipment and respect for safety. Our multi-storey window cleaning is built around methods that reduce risk for both our team and your occupants.

For many buildings, water-fed pole systems are the preferred option. These extend from the ground and deliver purified water directly to the glass, so there is no need to climb or lean out of upper windows. The pure water dissolves dirt, and the surface dries without streaks, which is ideal for large panes and facades that would be difficult to squeegee by hand.

Before we start a high-reach job, we look at access, surrounding hazards, weather conditions and any building requirements. If a particular building needs a different approach, we will explain what is recommended and why, rather than cutting corners to hit a price. For strata managers and building owners, this means you get a clear plan and a consistent result across every level.

Interior and Exterior Window Cleaning: Why Both Matter

A window has two faces, and each collects a different kind of dirt. Cleaning only one side often leaves the glass looking patchy, especially when sunlight hits it at an angle. That is why most of our customers choose to have both sides done together.

Interior Window Cleaning

Inside, glass picks up fingerprints, pet marks, dust, cooking vapour and the film that builds from heating and cooling. Our interior window cleaning removes these marks without soaking carpets or leaving residue on your sills. We take care around furniture, curtains and electronics, and we leave the area as tidy as we found it.

Exterior Window Cleaning

Outside, the conditions are tougher. Salt spray, traffic grime, bird droppings, pollen and mineral deposits from rain and sprinklers all attach to the glass. Our exterior window cleaning uses purified water and soft brushes to loosen and rinse away this build-up. The deionised water leaves no mineral residue, so the glass dries clear and stays cleaner for longer.

Frames, Tracks and Hard Water Stains

Clean glass sitting in a dirty frame never looks quite right. We detail frames, sills and sliding tracks to remove grit, cobwebs and dead insects that make windows stiff and unsightly. Where hard water stains are present, we assess the severity and use suitable methods to treat them. Some long-term staining can etch the glass permanently, so we will always be upfront about what can be improved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my windows cleaned in Sydney?

Most homes benefit from a clean every three to six months. Properties near the coast, busy roads or building sites usually need more frequent visits, while commercial shopfronts are often cleaned monthly or fortnightly.

What is water-fed pole window cleaning?

It uses a lightweight extendable pole fed with purified, deionised water. The pure water lifts dirt off the glass and dries spot-free, so no harsh chemicals or squeegee marks are left behind.

Can you remove hard water stains from glass?

In most cases, yes. We use safe stain removal methods to treat mineral deposits and sprinkler marks. Severe etching can be permanent, so we assess the glass first and explain what is achievable.

Do you clean high-rise and strata buildings?

Yes. We service apartment blocks, strata complexes and commercial towers, using high-reach systems suited to the building. Contact us with the property details and we will recommend the safest approach.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not always. Exterior cleaning can often be done without anyone home, provided we have safe access. Interior cleaning requires access to the property, which we can arrange in advance.

Do you offer one-off and regular window cleaning?

Yes. You can book a single clean, for example before a sale or after renovations, or set up a regular schedule that suits your property and budget.
